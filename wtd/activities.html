Co-Curricular & Student Life

Champions on the field, leaders on the parade ground, and innovators in the guild halls.

Sports & Athletics

All Year

First XI Cricket

A proud tradition competing in Sri Lanka Schools Cricket Association tournaments, regular division fixtures, and the celebrated Annual Big Match encounter.

Annual Season

Track & Field Athletics

Comprehensive training spanning sprints, middle distance, hurdles, jumps, and throws, yielding numerous zonal, provincial, and all-island championship medals.

Active Term

Volleyball & Netball

Fast-paced team sports building athletic endurance, strategic cohesion, and tournament triumphs in district and all-island school games.

Year Round

Chess & Indoor Sports

Sharpening tactical intellect and focus through competitive inter-school chess championships, badminton leagues, and table tennis tournaments.

Cadeting & Youth Leadership

6th NCC Battalion

National Cadet Corps (NCC) Platoons

Renowned for exemplary military discipline, precision drill commands, and survival endurance, consistently earning honours at the Rantembe National Assessment Camp.

Scout Association

College Scout Troop & Cub Scouts

Instilling outdoor survival skills, ethical character, first-aid capability, and selfless community service, producing coveted President's Scout Badge recipients.

Ceremonial Band

Western Brass Band & Eastern Orchestra

Providing magnificent ceremonial fanfare for assemblies, parades, sports meets, and winning laurels at All-Island Aesthetic Competitions.

Clubs, Societies & Guilds

Academic Guild

Science & Innovation Society

Organizing biennial science exhibitions, chemistry Olympiad workshops, rocketry demonstrations, and robotics discovery workshops.

Student Body

Media & Broadcasting Unit

The official voice of the college managing campus public address, photography, video production, live event streaming, and journalism.

Religious Guild

Buddhist Association (බෞද්ධ සංගමය)

Preserving spiritual harmony through morning pirith chanting, annual Vesak/Poson lanterns, Dhamma examinations, and charitable community outreach.

Commerce Guild

Commerce & Entrepreneurship Society

Fostering financial acumen, business case competitions, Young Leaders conferences, and the annual Commerce Day symposium.

Tech Club

Information Technology & Coding Guild

Equipping students with modern web development, Python programming, graphic design, and cybersecurity fundamentals.

Literary Unit

Debating & Literary Associations

Mastering public speaking, Parliamentary style debates in Sinhala and English, and publishing student anthologies.

Annual College Traditions

Term 1

Inter-House Sports Championship

A grand spectacle of track & field rivalry between Senanayake, Jayathilake, Gemunu, and Parakrama houses, featuring vibrant drill displays and march pasts.

Term 2

Annual College Prize Giving

The premier academic assembly of the year honoring scholastic distinctions, subject prizes, and highest national examination achievers.

Term 3

Colors Night & Sports Gala

Bestowing prestigious College Colors and special crests upon athletes and cadets who represented the college and Sri Lanka on national stages.
