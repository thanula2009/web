Welcome to Excellence

A Proud Legacy of Character, Wisdom & National Service

Founded in the historic educational hub of Mirigama, D.S. Senanayake Central College has flourished into one of the country's pre-eminent institutions. Inspired by the statesmanship and selflessness of Sri Lanka's Father of the Nation, our mission is to cultivate well-rounded global thinkers who hold fast to their traditional values.

Under our guiding principle "Sunath • Dhareth • Charatha" (Listen intently, Retain wisdom, Practice righteous conduct), students are nurtured through a rigorous academic curriculum, robust cadeting and sports programs, and transformative cultural leadership.
