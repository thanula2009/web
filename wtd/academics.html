Academic Overview

D.S. Senanayake Central College delivers the comprehensive Sri Lankan National Curriculum prescribed by the Ministry of Education, enriched with modern pedagogical strategies, active laboratory engagement, and digital learning tools. With a dedicated faculty of over 180 qualified and experienced educators, our educational philosophy fosters independent critical inquiry, bilingual fluency, and problem-solving skills. Year after year, our students achieve exceptional pass rates and island-level distinctions in both G.C.E. Ordinary Level and Advanced Level examinations, gaining entry to top national and international universities.

Academic Sections

• Primary Section (Grades 1 – 5): Child-centered foundational education focusing on core literacy, numeracy, environmental studies, and activity-based learning, culminating in specialized preparation for the National Grade 5 Scholarship Examination. • Junior Secondary (Grades 6 – 9): A broad exploratory curriculum introducing integrated sciences, mathematics, languages (Sinhala & English), history, geography, civics, health & physical education, information technology, and aesthetic arts (Art, Music, Dancing, and Drama). • Senior Secondary (Grades 10 – 11 / G.C.E. Ordinary Level): Focused preparation for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examinations. Students study core subjects alongside specialized elective baskets in Commerce, Technical subjects, and Humanities. • Collegiate Section (Grades 12 – 13 / G.C.E. Advanced Level): Pre-university education across five distinct academic streams supported by advanced laboratories, practical assessments, and comprehensive seminar series.

G.C.E. A/L Streams & Subjects

1. Physical Science Stream (Mathematical Science): Combined Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Information & Communication Technology (ICT). 2. Biological Science Stream: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Agricultural Science. 3. Commerce Stream: Accounting, Business Studies, Economics, and Information & Communication Technology (ICT). 4. Arts & Humanities Stream: Sinhala, Political Science, Buddhist Civilization, Logic & Scientific Method, History, Geography, and English Literature. 5. Technology Stream: Engineering Technology (ET), Biosystems Technology (BST), Science for Technology (SFT), and Information & Communication Technology (ICT).

Educational Facilities & Programs

• Advanced Science Laboratories: Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories enabling hands-on experimental research. • Computer Laboratories & Smart Classrooms: Modern ICT suites with broadband connectivity, interactive multimedia displays, and coding programs. • College Library & Resource Center: A vast repository of academic references, national periodicals, fiction, and peaceful study halls. • Bilingual & English Medium Education: Dedicated streams offering science and mathematics in the English medium from Grade 6 upwards. • Remedial Coaching & Examination Seminars: Target-oriented supplementary seminars led by master teachers and exam paper authors to support students of every academic tier.
