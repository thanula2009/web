Our History

Founded in 1947 during the transformative era of Sri Lanka's Free Education movement, D.S. Senanayake Central College, Mirigama was established under the visionary guidance of the Father of the Nation and first Prime Minister of independent Sri Lanka, the Rt. Hon. D. S. Senanayake. Rooted in the lush educational landscape of Gampaha District, the institution was conceived with a noble mission: to provide the highest caliber of secondary education to children regardless of their social background. Over nearly eight decades of illustrious service, the college has evolved into an esteemed National School, consistently producing distinguished scholars, physicians, engineers, military leaders, civil servants, entrepreneurs, and national athletes who have brought glory to the motherland. Today, while honoring our venerable traditions, our campus integrates state-of-the-art science laboratories, multimedia smart classrooms, an expansive sports complex, and an inspiring academic atmosphere catering to over 2,500 students.

School Identity

Motto (තේමා පාඨය): "Sunath • Dhareth • Charatha" (සැදැහැයෙන් අසව් • නුවණින් දරව් • දැහැමෙන් හැසිරෙව් — Listen intently, Retain wisdom, Practice righteous conduct). College Colours: Emerald Green and Gold (මරකත කොළ සහ රන්වන් පැහැය) — Representing life, vitality, resilience, honor, and supreme intellectual wisdom. College Houses (නිවාස ක්‍රමය): • Senanayake (රතු / Red) — Commemorating the Father of the Nation, Rt. Hon. D. S. Senanayake. • Jayathilake (කහ / Yellow) — Honoring Sir D. B. Jayathilake, great statesman and scholar. • Gemunu (නිල් / Blue) — Symbolizing valor, courage, and determination inspired by King Dutugemunu. • Parakrama (කොළ / Green) — Symbolizing prosperity, strategic intellect, and wisdom inspired by King Parakramabahu the Great.
